About CartShare

CartShare is a Collaborative Real-Time Group Shopping Platform where multiple users can create or join a shopping room, add products to a shared cart, and shop together in real time.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make online shopping more interactive by allowing friends, families, and teams to collaborate while shopping together.
